Official Privacy & Usage Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Applies to all MultiMail Studio services

1. Information We Collect & Process

MultiMail Studio is built on a strict data-minimization architecture. When you use the Mail Generator:

  • Smart Name Detection: Names typed into the generator (e.g., Alex Jack or Tawsif Ahamed Shuvo) are formatted locally in your browser to assist with signup forms.
  • Email Handles & Domains: Generated email addresses are registered in Firebase solely to prevent duplicate collisions and route incoming messages.
  • No Behavioral Profiling: We never sell, rent, or share user data with third-party advertising networks.

2. Real-Time Inbox & Message Retention

Your temporary inbox is designed for fast, frictionless verification and software QA testing:

  • OTP Parsing: Incoming 4–8 digit verification codes are detected client-side inside your browser for 1-click copying.
  • Instant Deletion: Clicking Delete Email or Clear Inbox immediately removes messages from your view and cloud inbox node.
  • Welcome Mail Auto-Clean: Clicking New Mail automatically removes the initial welcome message so your workspace stays uncluttered.

3. Browser LocalStorage & Preferences

Instead of tracking cookies, MultiMail Studio uses standard browser localStorage to remember your workspace state across refreshes:

  • Theme Mode: Stores your Light or Dark UI preference (multimail_theme_v2).
  • Domain Registry: Stores your active 15+ domains and chosen Default Domain (multimail_default_domain_v2).
  • Saved Mails & Password Helper: Keeps your generated email list and custom signup password accessible on your device until you clear them.

4. Acceptable Use & Security Policy

By using MultiMail Studio, you agree to abide by the following security and ethical rules:

  • Permitted Uses: Software QA automation, developer webhook testing, trial signups, and protecting your primary inbox from spam.
  • Prohibited Activities: Sending unsolicited bulk spam, phishing, unlawful fraud, or abusing API rate limits is strictly forbidden.
  • Sensitive Accounts Warning: Do not use temporary email addresses for banking, government IDs, or critical medical records.

5. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

To deliver real-time synchronization and instant customer support, MultiMail integrates with trusted cloud providers:

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database: Used for instant username availability checks and live message delivery.
  • Telegram & Google Sheets Support Sync: When you submit a ticket on the Contact Us page, your inquiry is forwarded to our support team.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Data Control

You retain full control over your generated addresses and local workspace data at all times:

  • You can delete individual emails, clear entire inboxes, or wipe your My Generated Mails list with a single click.
  • Click the Clear My Browser Data button above at any time to reset all local storage keys and restore factory defaults.
  • For custom domain takedowns or enterprise compliance inquiries, reach out via our Contact Us page.

Platform Documentation & Workflow

Full System Overview
01

Select Real-Time Live API or Demo Domain Mode

Open the Domains page to switch between Real-Time API Domains (powered by Maildrop, GrabMail, Mail.tm, Mail.gw & Tempmail+ for live external verification emails) and 15+ Demo Extensions for sandbox testing.

02

Configure Random Alias Persona (195+ Countries, Gender & Age)

Click the 3-line Settings icon next to Random Alias to choose from all 195 world countries with live country flags (10 visible per scroll), select Gender (Random, Male, Female), and drag the Age Progression Slider (18–65 yrs).

03

Smart Name Isolation, Dot (.) & Auto-Rerolling Numbers

Type or paste any name (even concatenated like alexjack) — MultiMail segments it into Alex Jack in the Entered Name box while formatting your email handle with optional First.Last Dot (.) and 1–8 random digits that automatically reroll on every new mail.

04

3-Second Fast Sync, Instant OTP & 1-Click Verification Links

Your active inbox syncs every 3 seconds (and immediately when you switch back to this browser tab). Incoming emails are deduplicated, rendered in clean HTML, and scanned for both 4–8 digit OTP codes and clickable Verify Email Now buttons.

05

Save Your Recovery Kit (MMK-XXXX-XXXX) & Scannable QR Code

Copy or download your .TXT Recovery Kit when creating a mail, or scan the real Address QR Code with any phone camera. Click Recover Email anytime to restore your live inbox session.
